Questions for the class

  • Which plan made the most money at the end of the plan? Which one after 20 years? Why are they different?
  • Which plans kept every rare animal? What did they do that others did not?
  • Which plans hurt the fewest workers, and kept the town’s water safe? What did that cost?
  • If groups played different lands, what lesson was the same everywhere, and what was different?
  • Did any group get unlucky with a spill? Was protecting the water worth its cost for them?
  • Is there a plan that is not beaten on both money and rare animals by any other plan? (Marked Best of both.)
