Simplified model with illustrative numbers. The air starts at sea level at 28 °C with a dew point of 20 °C. Unsaturated air cools 10 °C per km as it rises. Once it reaches 100% humidity (here at 1,000 m) it condenses and cools about 5 °C per km. Air descending the far side warms 10 °C per km. The crest is 3,000 m.
