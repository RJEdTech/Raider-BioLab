1. Read the soil profile

Dig a pit and you will see bands of different color and texture. These bands are called horizons. Select a layer to see what it is made of and what it does for plants.

Identify the horizon

Each clue describes one horizon. You get one graded attempt per question, then you will see why.

2. Mix a soil on the texture triangle

Soil texture is the share of three particle sizes: sand (0.05–2 mm, gritty), silt (0.002–0.05 mm, smooth and floury), and clay (under 0.002 mm, sticky). Every mixture lands on one spot in the triangle, and that spot gives it a name.

Drag the dot, or use the sliders. Read clay on the left edge, silt on the right edge, and sand along the bottom.

The three always add up to 100%. Moving one slider rebalances the other two.

3. Drainage lab: filtration and permeability

Infiltration is how fast water enters the soil surface. Permeability is how easily water moves through connected pores below it. Pour 200 mL of water onto each mixture and see how much drains out in 60 seconds.
